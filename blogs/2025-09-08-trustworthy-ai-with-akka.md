<p>Trustworthy AI is not a feature you can simply add at the end; it must be an inherent part of the framework's design. Akka is engineered from the ground up to provide the bedrock of trust, but it is a shared responsibility. While our platform offers the essential tools and architectural principles, organizations using Akka must actively configure and implement the controls necessary to build trustworthy AI applications.</p>
